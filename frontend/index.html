Our History

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Women's Institute of Technology (APJAKWIT), Darbhanga, was established in 2005. It is a premier technical institution of national importance, specifically designed for women. The institute is a constituent unit of L. N. Mithila University, Darbhanga.

Our Vision

To be a leading technical institute for women, fostering innovation, leadership, and ethical values. We strive to create an environment where women can excel in engineering and contribute to the technological advancement of the country.

About APJAKWIT

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Women's Institute of Technology (APJAKWIT) is a premier unit of Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), Darbhanga. Established with a vision to bridge the gender gap in technical education, we provide world-class training in CSE, IT, and MCA.
